# CreatorPantry — AI Pricing, Allowance and Billing SCRUB Prompts

Atomic prompts that close CreatorPantry's AI cost exposure, then add plans, prepaid credit packs and Stripe
billing on top of the allowance system Phase 9A already built. For use with Claude Code and the
CreatorPantry `.claude/` toolkit. Path in the repo: `docs/prompts/1.creatorpantry-ai-pricing-scrub-prompts.md`.

This document supplements `docs/prompts/creatorpantry-scrub-microprompts.md` (the "master library"). It does
not redefine anything decided in `CLAUDE.md` or `.claude/rules/`, except where AP.1.1 amends `CLAUDE.md`'s
out-of-bounds list on the owner's explicit request. The pricing analysis behind it is
`creatorpantry-ai-pricing.md` (project files, 2026-10-24); AP.1.1 copied the parts it needs into the repo as
`docs/architecture-decisions/ai-pricing.md` (B-44–B-54), which is now the record to cite.

## Reusable SCRUB skeleton

```text
SCOPE:        one observable change and the exact repository seam it touches
CONSTRAINT:   stack, rule files, skills, requirement IDs, and prerequisites
RESTRICTION:  explicit exclusions and invariants that must not be weakened
UTILIZATION:  skills, read-only reviewers, and tools to invoke
BEHAVIOR:     inspect, plan, wait for approval, implement, test, and report
```

Run one prompt at a time, in order, in a clean context where practical. Mark a prompt `- done` only after
its stated verification passes, and add a delivery note under it when what shipped differs from what was
asked.

## What this answers

The owner asked on 2026-10-24 for "a pricing structure and overage AI structure so I don't lose money on
image and/or content creation", and for a faster image model.

| ID | Requirement | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PRICE-001 | Every provider call that costs money, text or image, is charged against the account's allowance before it runs. | 2 |
| PRICE-002 | Credits are a pinned unit: 1 credit = $0.001 of provider list cost × 1.25. Rates are configuration, validated at startup. | 2 |
| PRICE-003 | Image requests name their quality and resolution explicitly; nothing is left to a provider default. | 2 |
| PRICE-004 | A creator sees what an image request will cost, in credits, before submitting it. | 2 |
| PRICE-005 | A platform-wide daily spend ceiling stops new image admissions and alerts the operator. | 2 |
| PRICE-006 | Images come in quality classes (draft, standard, high, high-2K, high-4K), each mapped to a configured model and setting. | 3 |
| PRICE-007 | An account has exactly one plan at a time; the plan sets the allowance, daily image cap, image concurrency and allowed quality classes. | 4 |
| PRICE-008 | Prepaid credit packs add to the allowance, are spent after it, and do not expire (B-50; first written as 12 months). | 5 |
| PRICE-009 | There is no post-paid overage. An account at zero is refused at admission, as USAGE-006 already does. | 5 |
| PRICE-010 | Subscriptions and packs are bought through Stripe; webhooks are verified and idempotent. | 6 |
| PRICE-011 | When an account's credits run low the creator is told and offered a pack; nothing is ever bought for them (amended by B-59; first: opt-in auto top-up). | 6 |
| PRICE-012 | Trial accounts cannot generate unbounded images. | 7 |
| PRICE-013 | Every published picture price — quote, picker, landing page, billing page — is the rate that request is charged at, per quality class, read from one table (added 2026-10-27, B-55). | 3, 4 |

## Decisions already made

1. **Billing, plans and metering are now in scope.** `CLAUDE.md` lists them as out of bounds "unless
   explicitly requested"; the owner requested them on 2026-10-24. AP.1.1 records that.
2. **Images are the cost, text is not.** A text action on `gpt-4o-mini` costs under $0.01; one image at
   today's settings costs $0.07. Phase 2 is ordered so the image leak closes first and needs no billing.
3. **Prepaid only.** No metered overage invoiced after the fact.
4. **Allowance stays per Identity account** (USAGE-001), across every workspace the account works in. A plan
   attaches to the account, not to a workspace.
5. **The trial is 14 days.** Owner, 2026-10-24, answering LP.2.
6. **Packs do not expire** (B-50). Owner, 2026-10-24 (LP.8), confirmed 2026-10-26. This amends PRICE-008.
7. **The trial gate is a lifetime cap of 10 pictures, with no card on file** (B-52). Owner, 2026-10-26.
8. **Pictures stay on Venice** (B-53). AP.3.4 is skipped. Owner, 2026-10-26.
9. **Downgrades, cancellations and a trial running out take effect at period end** (B-54). Owner, 2026-10-26.
   How an upgrade lands mid-period is open (`ai-pricing.md`, Open items), settled before AP.4.2.
10. **A picture's price is the rate it is charged at** (B-55). Owner, 2026-10-27. A quality class costs
    `Ai:Quota:ImageRates` for the model, quality and resolution it sends; the quote, the picker, the landing page
    and the billing page all read that, and `Billing:Rates` loses its picture entries. AP.3.1 was amended and
    AP.3.1A and AP.4.5 added for it.
11. **Stripe Managed Payments is the merchant of record** (B-58) and **auto top-up becomes a low-balance notice**
    (B-59): nothing is ever bought for a creator; running low brings a notice offering a pack. Owner, 2026-10-28.
    PRICE-011 and AP.6.4 were rewritten for it.

## AP.2.2 Output-token ceiling per AI task - done

```text

RESTRICTION: No per-call override from the request body. The quota estimate per call must stay at or above
```

*Delivery note (2026-10-26): shipped, with the ceiling sent under a different name than the SDK would use.*

*Where it lives: `Ai:Gateway` (`AiGatewayOptions`) — `MaxOutputTokens` 4,000 and `MaxOutputTokensByTask`
(`LongFormOutline`, `LongFormSection` 8,000). Neither options class owned per-task settings; the gateway's is the
one the gateway reads. `AiCompletionRequest` gained a `required Task`, so all 17 handlers name theirs and the
compiler refuses one that does not. Nothing on a request can name a ceiling.*

*Sent as `max_completion_tokens`. Azure.AI.Inference 1.0.0-beta.5 maps `ChatOptions.MaxOutputTokens` to
`max_tokens`, which the GPT-5 family (the owner's `gpt-5.4-nano`) refuses with a 400 — the prompt as written
would have failed every call. The domain still sets the neutral `MaxOutputTokens`; `MaxCompletionTokensChatClient`
in `CreatorPantry.AiProvider` moves it into the request body as `max_completion_tokens`, and
`MaxCompletionTokensChatClientTests` drives the real SDK over a captured HTTP exchange to prove the body carries
that and no `max_tokens`. On a reasoning model the ceiling bounds hidden reasoning as well as the answer.*

*Truncation: a response the provider ends for length is `ai.output.truncated` (`OutputSchemaInvalid`,
correctable) and is never handed to a validator, even when what came back parses. It gets the one correction,
which says it was cut off; cut off twice, the run fails as truncated.*

*Estimate check: `AddCreatorPantryAi` refuses to start, in every environment, when a task's ceiling × the chat
model's output rate exceeds its per-call estimate, naming the task and figures. Input is not counted — the ceiling
does not bound it. At `gpt-5.4-nano` 4,000 tokens is 6.25 credits (under the default 10) and 8,000 is 12.5, so
`Ai:Quota:TaskEstimates` sets both long-form tasks to 15 (owner: "raise the cap") in the API and Worker. That
sizes the hold only; the charge is still what the call used.*

*Live run, against the owner's Azure `gpt-5.4-nano` deployment (local dev, at the owner's instruction), through
`AddCreatorPantryAi` and the real gateway: `LiveChannelPostsCheck`, `LiveLongFormOutlineCheck` and
`LiveLongFormSectionCheck` all passed — 15 calls, every one accepted with `max_completion_tokens` and every one
ending `stop`. **Nothing was truncated**; the longest answer was 705 output tokens against an 8,000 ceiling,
and the largest channel-posts answer 426 against 4,000. Each reported `gpt-5.4-nano-2026-03-17`, the dated id
AP.2.1's `AiModelRateKeys` exists for. Settled at the AP.2.1 rates, a channel-posts call is 1.0–1.4 credits, a
long-form outline 1.5–1.9 and a section 1.1–1.4 — the measured before/after AP.2.1 could not take: each of
those settled at the flat 10 before. Reasoning was light on these prompts; the menu's "about 5" and "about 10"
have ample room. Recorded by `Ai/Live/LiveUsage.cs` (`CP_LIVE_USAGE`), counts and ids only.*

*The offline evaluation sets ran in the Ai classes and pass; they are recorded outputs, so a ceiling cannot
truncate them — the Live run above is where truncation would show. Ai, AiUsage, Billing, OpenAPI and boundary
classes: 3,370 passed, 6 skipped (Live, run separately above), 0 failed.*

*ai-safety-reviewer found no blocker: no input reaches the ceiling, the gateway is the only `IChatClient` caller,
