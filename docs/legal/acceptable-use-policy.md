CreatorPantry is a workspace for food creators. This policy says what you may not do with it, so the Service
stays useful, safe and fair for everyone. It applies to everything you upload, generate, store or publish
through CreatorPantry, and to how you use the Service itself.

## 1. Content you may not upload, generate or publish

- **Anything illegal**, or anything that promotes or helps someone break the law.
- **Sexual content involving minors**, in any form. We report it to the authorities.
- **Sexually explicit or pornographic material.** Picture generation runs with safety filtering on; do not
  try to get around it.
- **Harassment, threats, hate** or content that demeans people for who they are.
- **Violence or self-harm** promoted or glorified.
- **Someone else's work you don't have the rights to**: photographs, recipes, articles, logos or brand
  material copied without permission or a lawful basis.
- **Impersonation**: content that pretends to be another person, creator, brand or organisation, or a picture
  of a real, identifiable person made to look like a real event.
- **Private information** about other people, such as addresses, phone numbers or ID documents, without their
  consent.
- **Malware**, phishing or anything designed to damage devices or steal data.

## 2. Food, health and safety claims

CreatorPantry never certifies a dish, and you may not use it to tell your audience otherwise. Do not use the
Service to publish:

- claims that a dish is free of an allergen, safe for a medical condition, or suitable for a specific person,
  unless you have verified them yourself;
- AI-generated nutrition figures presented as a lab-tested or label value;
- preservation, canning, fermentation or cooking-temperature instructions you have not checked against a
  trusted source;
- medical, weight-loss or health claims presented as fact.

## 3. Publishing and your audience

- Don't use scheduling or publishing to send spam, bulk unsolicited messages, or misleading posts.
- Follow the rules of every platform you publish to, including their rules on disclosing sponsorships and
  AI-generated content.
- Don't publish AI-generated pictures of food in a way meant to deceive, such as passing a generated picture off
  as a review photo of a real restaurant dish. [OWNER TO DECIDE: whether to require disclosure of AI-generated
  pictures.]

## 4. Using the Service fairly

- **No getting around limits.** Don't bypass credits, daily picture limits, rate limits, plan restrictions or
  the Owner's AI switches, including by creating multiple accounts or trials.
- **No sharing accounts.** One person per account. Give others access through a workspace, not your password.
- **No automated access** except through features we provide: no scraping, bots or bulk account creation.
- **No attacking the Service**: no probing, scanning or testing its security without our written permission,
  no overloading it, and no trying to reach another workspace's data.
- **No tricking the AI**: don't feed it instructions designed to make it ignore its safeguards, reveal other
  people's data, or produce content this policy prohibits.
- **No reselling** the Service or its AI output as a competing AI service without our written agreement.

## 5. What happens if this policy is broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may remove or disable content, switch off features for a workspace,
suspend or end an account, or report it to the authorities. Where reasonable, we will tell you what happened
and give you a chance to fix it first. Credits used in breach of this policy are not refunded.
