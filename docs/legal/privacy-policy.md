## 2. What we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account details:** your email address and password. We store your password only as a secure hash, never
  as text *(product fact)*.
- **Workspace details:** the workspaces you create or belong to, and your role in each.
- **Your content:** recipes and their versions, notes, brand settings and documents, uploaded pictures,
  prompts you write, AI drafts and proposals, social posts, long-form pieces, board cards and tasks.
- **Pictures and their metadata.** We keep your original uploads exactly as you sent them, including their
  embedded metadata (EXIF), which can include **the location where a photo was taken**. The smaller versions we
  show in the app have that metadata removed *(product fact)*. Downloading an original gives you the file with
  its metadata intact.
- **Messages to us**, such as support emails.
- **Payment details** [WHEN BILLING SHIPS]: payments will be processed by [Stripe]. We will receive your plan,
  billing status and the last four digits and expiry of your card, never the full card number.

### Information created when you use the Service

- **AI usage:** for each AI request, the feature used, the model, the prompt-template version, how many tokens
  and credits it used, how long it took and whether it succeeded. We record this **without the contents of
  your prompt** in our logs *(product fact)*. Your account's credit balance and what it was spent on are shown
  to you on your usage page.
- **Activity records:** an audit trail of important actions in a workspace — who did what, to which item, and
  when — so a workspace can see its own history and we can investigate problems *(product fact)*.
- **Publishing records** [WHERE PUBLISHING IS AVAILABLE]: the posts you schedule, their target profiles, times,
  delivery status and the provider's reference for each.
- **Technical information:** your IP address, browser type, request times, and error and performance data.
  We use IP addresses to limit repeated sign-in attempts and to keep the Service secure *(product fact)*.

## 5. AI features

AI features are off until a workspace's Owner switches them on *(product fact)*. When you use one:

- The text you send — and the recipe, brand guide or other workspace content the feature needs — goes to our
  AI provider to produce a result for you.
- Only your own workspace's content is ever used. We never ground a request with another workspace's
  examples or private data *(product fact)*.
- Content you upload or import is treated as material to work on, not as instructions to the AI.
- What the AI produces is saved in your workspace as a draft or proposal until you accept or decline it.
