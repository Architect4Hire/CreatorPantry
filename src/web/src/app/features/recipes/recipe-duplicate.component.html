What comes across

  • Ingredients, method, equipment and tags
  • Times, yield, notes and attribution
  • Media links — pointing at the same files, which are not copied or re-owned

What does not

  • The version history: the copy starts its own at version 1
  • The source's status: a copy is always a Draft
  • Authorship and timestamps: the copy is yours, made now
