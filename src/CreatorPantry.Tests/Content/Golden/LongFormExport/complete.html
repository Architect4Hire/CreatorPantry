Buttermilk soda bread, start to finish

The loaf for people who do not bake

Some breads ask for a whole afternoon. This one asks for a bowl, a spoon and an oven that is already hot.

From bowl to oven

Mix the dry ingredients first.

Stir in the buttermilk until the dough only just comes together.
Then bake for 45 minutes.

If the top browns too fast

Cover it loosely and carry on.

Three things that help

  • Stir until the dough only just holds.
  • Have the oven hot first.
  • Use the flour I use.

My grandmother's rule: a wet dough and a hot oven.

  1. Weigh.
  2. Mix.
  3. Bake.
