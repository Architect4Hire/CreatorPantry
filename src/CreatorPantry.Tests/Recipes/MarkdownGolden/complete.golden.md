# Buttermilk Soda Bread

> A dense, tangy loaf.

*Maeve Byrne* · <https://example.com/soda-bread>

**Prep** 15 min · **Cook** 45 min · **Total** 1 h 30 min · **Yield** Makes 2 loaves

Adapted from my grandmother's table.

## Headnote (editorial)

Simple and forgiving.

## Introduction (editorial)

No yeast needed.

## Equipment

- Dutch oven
- Pastry brush (optional)

## Ingredients

### Dry

- 500 g plain flour
- 1 tsp salt

### Wet

- 400 ml buttermilk
- 1 egg (optional)

## Instructions

### Dough

1. Mix the dry ingredients.
2. Add the buttermilk.

### Bake

1. Bake until hollow-sounding.

## Notes

Do not overmix.

## Storage

Keeps 2 days.

## Tips (editorial)

- Use cold buttermilk.

## Substitutions (editorial)

- **500 g plain flour** — Use wholemeal flour. Denser crumb.

## Storage and reheating (editorial)

Reheat in a hot oven.

## FAQ (editorial)

**Can I freeze it?**

Yes, sliced.
